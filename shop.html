Before you start

Can I make a baby memory book without photos?
Yes. Use a letter or a notes page and write a few sentences. You can mix photo pages and photo-free pages in the same book.
Do I need Canva or an app?
No. You make and preview the book in your browser. It isn’t a blank PDF you have to fill in or a template you have to open in another app.
What do I get, and can I print it?
You download the book you made as an A4 or US Letter PDF. Print it at home or ask a copy shop for standard document printing. A custom bound photo book may need a different file.
Is it a subscription?
No. Try the editor free, then pay $9.90 USD once through PayPal when you want your finished PDF.
How do I get my PDF again?
Open the editor in the same browser and choose Recover order. Save a copy of your download before switching devices or clearing browser data. Need help? Email hello@citymomnotes.com with your order number and a short description. Please don’t send family photos or payment details.
