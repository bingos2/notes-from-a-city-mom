When do I start setting limits?

Why toddlers push back, what to say, and how to save your energy.

Hi,

I’m writing this with one hand on my nose.

My baby headbutted me right in the face—and then laughed like it was the funniest thing in the world. I saw stars. My baby had a great time.

Meanwhile, I had one question: When do words actually start working?

My mother-in-law says words won’t solve everything, even when they’re older. Maybe she’s right. But surely my nose deserves a break someday.

When do toddlers push back?

When something unsafe is taken away. When playtime ends. When they want to do it all by themselves. Hunger, tiredness, and transitions can make things harder, too.
