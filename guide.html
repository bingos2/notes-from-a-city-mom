Free guide · 15 months

My Toddler's Little Wins.

A gentle way to notice what your toddler is learning, trying, and doing—without turning everyday life into a test.

Made from the CDC’s official 15-month milestone checklist, then translated into a warm, easy-to-use parent guide.

What this guide is

A simple companion for the moments you already share: playtime, meals, reading, getting dressed, and those tiny “did you see that?” wins. It helps you notice developmental milestones in four areas: social and emotional connection, language and communication, learning and problem-solving, and movement.

What you’ll find inside

  • A clear, parent-friendly version of the CDC’s 15-month milestones.
  • Small everyday examples, such as showing you a favorite object, pointing to ask for help, stacking two small objects, taking a few independent steps, or using fingers to feed themselves.
  • Space to celebrate what you are already seeing and jot down what you want to ask at your child’s next visit.
  • Two downloads: a mobile-friendly version and a print version sized for A4 or US Letter.

How to use it

Read it slowly—there is no score to earn. Notice what feels familiar, celebrate the little things, and use it as a starting point for a conversation with your child’s doctor if you have a question. Development varies; this guide is not a developmental screening tool or medical advice.
