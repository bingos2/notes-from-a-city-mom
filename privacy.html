Privacy · Effective October 22, 2026

Your email is for the notes.

This policy explains how Notes from a City Mom handles the information you share when you subscribe or request a guide.

Information we collect

We collect the email address you provide when you subscribe to our newsletter or request a digital guide. We do not ask for or knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

How we use it

We use your email to send the newsletter you chose to receive and, when available, the guide you requested. We do not sell or share your email for advertising.

Service providers

We use Resend to store subscriber contact information and deliver email. Resend processes information only to provide that service to us.

Retention and your choices

We keep your email while you remain subscribed. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in an email. To request deletion of your email, contact us at hello@citymomnotes.com. We retain a minimal record of unsubscribed addresses for up to two years only to honor opt-out requests and prevent accidental resubscription.

Cookies and analytics

We do not currently use advertising pixels or third-party analytics cookies. Our hosting provider may use essential technical processing needed to deliver and secure the website.
