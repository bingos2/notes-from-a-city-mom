Free printable guide · 15 months

My Toddler's Little Wins.

A gentle little record for noticing what your 15-month-old is learning, trying, and doing - without turning everyday life into a test.

Built around the CDC’s 15-Month Milestone Moments Checklist.

A4 printable PDF · Newsletter is optional · Unsubscribe anytime.

01

Notice the little wins.

Look through familiar moments - playing, reading, meals, cuddles, and moving around - and mark what you have already noticed.

02

Keep a parent record.

There is room for first-noticed dates, questions, and the small things you want to remember for your child’s next checkup.

03

Know what to do next.

No score to add up. If something concerns you or a skill is lost, the guide points you toward talking with your child’s doctor.

A peek inside

Made to be used,
not just saved.

Each section turns milestones into familiar, everyday observations - with space to make the guide your own.

More than a checklist

A place for the things you notice.

Save a new word, a favorite thing, a question for the doctor, or a tiny moment you loved. These are the details that disappear quickly - and matter later.

A gentle reminder

There is no score to earn.

Children grow at different paces. This is a parent record, not a developmental screening or diagnosis. If you have concerns, if one or more milestones are not yet happening, or if your child has lost skills, talk with your child’s doctor and ask about developmental screening.

Source: CDC: Milestones by 15 Months
