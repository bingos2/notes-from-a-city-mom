What to Write in a Baby Memory Book When You’re Starting Late

You don’t have to start with the day your baby was born. Start with one little moment you can still picture. A complete page can be three sentences: what happened, one detail that made it feel like your child, and what you want to remember.

Three baby memory book prompts

  1. What made them laugh?“You laughed every time the spoon fell. By the fifth drop, we were both laughing.”
  2. What did they do with their hands?“You put a blueberry in each hand, then held one out to me.”
  3. What did the room sound like?“I could hear your little feet in the hall before I saw you come around the corner.”

A finished page with no photo

A letter about an ordinary moment
Today you laughed so hard at a bubble landing on your toes. We sat on the kitchen floor and blew a hundred more. I wish I could bottle that sound.

What if you can’t remember the date?

Write “around your first birthday,” “that summer,” or leave the date off. You can use a photo to jog your memory, but you don’t need one to tell the story.
