About

Practical parenting help, written with care.

Notes from a City Mom is a growing library of clear, age-relevant ideas for the everyday moments when you want to know what to say or try next.

Our editorial approach

Each note is designed to be useful in a real week: one small idea, plain language, and examples you can use with your child. Where a topic draws on child-development or health guidance, the note identifies its source so you can read further.

What this site is—and is not

These notes are educational and are not a substitute for medical, developmental, legal, or mental-health advice. If you are concerned about your child’s health, development, safety, or wellbeing, speak with a qualified professional who knows your family.

Sources and updates

Sources are listed with individual notes when relevant. Guides are updated as the library grows; availability by age is shown clearly so you can see what is ready now and what is still in development.
