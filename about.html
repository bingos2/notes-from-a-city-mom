About

Practical parenting help, written with care.

Notes from a City Mom shares clear, useful ideas for the ordinary moments of life with little ones.

Our approach

Each note starts with real life: one small idea, plain language, and something you can try today. When a topic draws on child-development or health guidance, we link the source so you can read more.

A gentle note

These resources are educational and not a substitute for medical, developmental, legal, or mental-health advice. If you’re concerned about your child, speak with a qualified professional who knows your family.
